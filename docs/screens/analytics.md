Two tabs over a date range (the selected tab, scope and range are remembered). **Usage**: tokens, cost and session metrics per day, provider, model and project, for the orchestrator's runs or every CLI session on this machine; "Ask the supervisor" writes a report on the range (a real model call). **Repository performance**: per project, how well its repository takes parallel runs: the files most runs edit (runs that edited each file, % of runs, its line count today; generated files marked), the share of runs that hit a merge conflict (the `resolve_merge` / `resolve_conflict` step or a failed `gh pr merge` / `glab mr merge`) and their share of the cost, with the list of those runs and the files their conflict named, the runs and roadmap stages active at once per day and the hours runs were active. A marker (a date or a commit) splits every number into before and after, to check the targets of a change such as epic #158. Computed offline from the run folders, the index and the local clone's `git log`: no model call, no network
