The Oracle's one conversation for the whole app (see The Oracle below): docked on the Dashboard, and a drawer on every other screen from the **Oracle** button at the bottom left of the sidebar, whose badge counts the answers that arrived while it was closed. Answers render Markdown and tables, with their time, model, cost and tokens; run ids, issues and run files open in the app; pasted screenshots are sent with the question; **Memory** edits its standing decisions and **New session** starts over from a handoff note. The footer shows today's spend against the daily budget (Settings › Execution); paused by budget or answering in another app process, the input is replaced by a notice
