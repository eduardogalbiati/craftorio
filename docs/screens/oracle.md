The Oracle's one conversation for the whole app (see The Oracle below): docked on Home (the Dashboard), and a drawer on every other screen from the **Oracle** button at the top right of the header (#254, next to the notifications bell), whose badge counts the answers that arrived while it was closed. Answers render Markdown and tables, with their time, model, cost and tokens; run ids, issues and run files open in the app; pasted screenshots are sent with the question; **Memory** edits its standing decisions and **New session** starts over from a handoff note. The footer shows today's spend against the daily budget (Settings › Execution); paused by budget or answering in another app process, the input is replaced by a notice. While it answers, the chat shows what it is doing (an activity line with a timer, its last steps), then the answer as it is written; **Stop** ends the turn and keeps what was written, marked Stopped (#180)
