The work items of the project chosen in the header, read from its repository's `docs/work/<item>/` (#647, [spec](../work/write-doc/spec.md)): each with its stage and its documents (`intent.md`, `spec.md`, each with its `pt-BR` translation) and their status (**Draft**, **Accepted**, **Signed off**). **New item** asks for a title, creates the intent from the playbook's skeleton (one commit) and starts the `write-doc` workflow: the writer drafts it, then the run waits at its `human_edit` gate. The editor draws the document in a sandboxed page without scripts; select a passage and the chip above the writer's chat names it; ask for a change (paste images too): each request is one writer turn whose lines replace the selection in the source and its translation, **one commit per request**. **Changes by request** lists them with the count of unreviewed changes: **Mark reviewed**, or **Undo this change** (a revert commit; the later requests stay). Only **Send to review** ends the edit: the reviewer anchors its notes to the sections, then the approval gate offers **Accept intent** or **Sign off spec** (the front matter of both languages changes, with the approver and the date, one commit) or **Back to the edit**. An accepted intent offers **Write the spec** on its item. A signed-off spec offers **Create epic and phase issues** (the Backlog) and **Start the Build** (the Roadmap): nothing is filed or started before that click.
