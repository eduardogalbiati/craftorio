The upstream board of the project chosen in the header (#257): the discovery cards accepted in the Inbox, worked on until they are clear enough to commit, in the columns **Triage → Shape → Define → Ready**. A card shows its title, type, age in the column, clarity score and estimate when known, and its attachment and thread counts; it moves by drag and drop (also within a column, to order it) or with its **Move to…** menu. **WIP limits**: Shape and Define show `n / limit` (5 by default; the pencil in the header changes it); a move past the limit asks to confirm and the header turns amber, nothing is blocked. **Fast lane**: bug and hotfix cards run in the swimlane at the top and may go from Triage straight to Define (another card that skips Shape asks first). **Filters**: type, tag, author, and a search over titles and documents. A click opens the card in a side panel: type and tags, the document (**Preview** / **Edit**, then **Save** / **Cancel**), the acceptance criteria, attachments (drop or paste to add), the thread, and the **version history** (a version on each move, restore and run result: **Open** or **Restore**; the replaced document is kept as a version). In Shape, **Open in Write & Review** and **Open in AI Arena** prefill that screen with the card's document; when the run finishes, its document comes back to the card as a new version and a thread entry. **Clarity** (#166): every card has a score from 0 to 100% (`clarity 72%`, amber below the bar), computed from a rubric for its type (problem, goal, scope, non-goals, verifiable acceptance criteria, decisions, open questions, dependencies, evidence; plus users and success metrics for a PRD, alternatives considered for an RFC, steps to reproduce with observed and expected for a bug or hotfix: the `clarity-score` skill) each time its document, acceptance criteria or type change; the panel lists the missing points, who scored it and when. **The bar**: a card enters Ready only at **85%** or more (**Bar and night…** above the columns changes it per project, with an optional bar per type). Below it the move is refused and the card offers **Grill me**, an interview in its thread that asks one missing point at a time, proposes an answer when the card already holds one and writes each answer into the card until it reaches the bar or you end it, and **Move anyway**, which needs a reason and is recorded on the card with its score. A card already in Ready is never moved back. **Ready forecast**: above the columns, how much work is ready as nights of agent work (`2 nights ready (about 19 h of agent work, an estimate)`), counting only the Ready cards at or above the bar, from their estimates and the hours of your night (23:00 to 08:00 by default). **Esc** closes the card
