The upstream board of the project chosen in the header (#257): the discovery cards accepted in the Inbox, worked on until they are clear enough to commit, in the columns **Triage → Shape → Define → Ready**. A card shows its title, type, age in the column, clarity score and estimate when known, and its attachment and thread counts; it moves by drag and drop (also within a column, to order it) or with its **Move to…** menu. **WIP limits**: Shape and Define show `n / limit` (5 by default; the pencil in the header changes it); a move past the limit asks to confirm and the header turns amber, nothing is blocked. **Fast lane**: bug and hotfix cards run in the swimlane at the top and may go from Triage straight to Define (another card that skips Shape asks first). **Filters**: type, tag, author, and a search over titles and documents. A click opens the card in a side panel: type and tags, the document (**Preview** / **Edit**, then **Save** / **Cancel**), the acceptance criteria, attachments (drop or paste to add), the thread, and the **version history** (a version on each move, restore and run result: **Open** or **Restore**; the replaced document is kept as a version). In Shape, **Open in Write & Review** and **Open in AI Arena** prefill that screen with the card's document; when the run finishes, its document comes back to the card as a new version and a thread entry. **Clarity** (#166): every card has a score from 0 to 100% (`clarity 72%`, amber below the bar), computed from a rubric for its type (problem, goal, scope, non-goals, verifiable acceptance criteria, decisions, open questions, dependencies, evidence; plus users and success metrics for a PRD, alternatives considered for an RFC, steps to reproduce with observed and expected for a bug or hotfix: the `clarity-score` skill) each time its document, acceptance criteria or type change; the panel lists the missing points, who scored it and when. **The bar**: a card enters Ready only at **85%** or more (**Bar and night…** above the columns changes it per project, with an optional bar per type). Below it the move is refused and the card offers **Grill me**, an interview in its thread that asks one missing point at a time, proposes an answer when the card already holds one and writes each answer into the card until it reaches the bar or you end it, and **Move anyway**, which needs a reason and is recorded on the card with its score. A card already in Ready is never moved back. **Ready forecast**: above the columns, how much work is ready as nights of agent work (`2 nights ready (about 19 h of agent work, an estimate)`), counting only the Ready cards at or above the bar, from their estimates and the hours of your night (23:00 to 08:00 by default). **Commit to the backlog** (#258): the commitment point. A Ready card with a clarity of **85%** or more is committed from its panel; under 85% (a card moved to Ready anyway, or a type with a lower bar) the button is disabled, the panel lists the gaps and offers **Grill me**, which interviews a Ready card up to 85%. The dialog shows the Product Owner's proposed split, one row per issue (title, complexity, workflow and the tasks taken from the acceptance criteria, all editable; **merge with issue n** joins two) and **Plan them in the roadmap, in this order**; **File the issues** files them on the project's repository, each starting with `From discovery card <id>` and carrying its tasks and the card's document as evidence (in simulation-only mode they only join the workspace's backlog and the split is rule-based: no model, no gh/glab; in developer mode the split is rule-based too and **File the issues** only previews the commands: nothing is filed, the card stays in Ready). While the issues are being filed the dialog cannot be cancelled. A committed card leaves the columns for the **Committed** list beside the board and follows its issues: **In delivery** with the counts to do, running, merged locally and done and each issue's Backlog column, **Delivered** when all are done, **Released** with the version once a release shipped them. It never returns to the board and its panel is read-only; **New card from this one** starts new learning as a new Inbox card linked to it. **Esc** closes the card
