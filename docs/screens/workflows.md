The menu's **Workflows** group has one entry per tab of the screen (#254): Definitions, Runs, Builder and Pipelines. **Runs**: every run, live or finished (**Open chat** replays one), with the runs' **Artifacts** as its second tab; an expanded run shows its sessions, then its **Artifacts** (Produced first, **Show all** for its notes and logs, chips by category and kind) beside its **Repository** (the branch and its state, the commits newest first, **Review changes**). **Pipelines**: the saved pipelines alone. The header's **Supervisor** button opens the Supervisor's chat as a drawer. **Definitions**: the YAMLs in `<workspace>/workflows/` with their steps, agents/models, skills, `/goal`, `/loop`, parallel steps and branches. **Builder**: draw a workflow instead of writing YAML (see [Visual builder](#visual-builder)). Each definition is marked **default** (untouched copy of a workflow bundled with the app), **modified** (a default you edited; **Restore default** moves your copy to the trash and restores the bundled one, and **newer default** means the app ships a newer version than the one you edited) or **custom** (created by you). **Keep awake** (the switch in the header of Runs and Definitions, the same setting as Settings › Execution, per workspace, off by default): while a run is in progress, a roadmap is running or a Backlog queue has items to start, the app keeps the computer from sleeping (a run waiting at a gate counts only when a roadmap or a queue continues after it). The switch shows **Active: 2 runs, roadmap stage 3/6**, **On: waiting for work** or **Off**, and the sidebar shows **Keeping awake** while it holds; the display may still turn off, and choosing Sleep, closing the lid or the Windows power plan still apply
