Three sub-items in the menu (clicking Workflows opens **Runs**). **Runs**: every run, live or finished (**Open chat** replays one). **Definitions**: the YAMLs in `<workspace>/workflows/` with their steps, agents/models, skills, `/goal`, `/loop`, parallel steps and branches. **Builder**: draw a workflow instead of writing YAML (see [Visual builder](#visual-builder)). Each definition is marked **default** (untouched copy of a workflow bundled with the app), **modified** (a default you edited; **Restore default** moves your copy to the trash and restores the bundled one, and **newer default** means the app ships a newer version than the one you edited) or **custom** (created by you)
