The menu's **Workflows** group has one entry per tab of the screen (#254): Definitions, Runs, Builder and Pipelines. **Runs**: every run, live or finished, with the runs' **Artifacts** as its second tab; an expanded run shows its run id, then at the right **Resume from step…** (resumable runs), **◆ Send to supervisor** (the Supervisor's chat opens with the run attached; nothing is sent until you write and send, and the button waits while the Supervisor answers) and **Open run** (its Run screen, always on the Details tab; the Chat tab replays it) (#409), its sessions, then its **Artifacts** (Produced first, **Show all** for its notes and logs, chips by category and kind) beside its **Repository** (the branch and its state, the commits newest first, **Review changes**). **Pipelines**: the saved pipelines alone. The round **Supervisor** button at the bottom right of every tab opens the Supervisor's chat as a drawer (#409). **Definitions**: the YAMLs in `<workspace>/workflows/` with their steps, agents/models, skills, `/goal`, `/loop`, parallel steps and branches. **Builder**: draw a workflow instead of writing YAML (see [Visual builder](#visual-builder)). Each definition is marked **default** (untouched copy of a workflow bundled with the app), **modified** (a default you edited; **Restore default** moves your copy to the trash and restores the bundled one, and **newer default** means the app ships a newer version than the one you edited) or **custom** (created by you). **Download** on every card saves its YAML as it is on disk, named `<file>.yaml`; **Import file** (or a YAML dropped on the tab) and **Import URL** (https only) bring one in: the app validates it first, then a dialog lists what it does (steps, agents and their tools, `action:` commands, `ship`, `requires`) and reminds you that a workflow is code that runs on this machine; **Import** writes it as **custom** under the name you give (an existing name only with **Replace** ticked), **Cancel** writes nothing, and a workflow the loader rejects or that requires a newer app shows the reasons instead (#488). The screen opened with `?import=<url>` starts that import, the link a public page can offer. **Keep awake** (the switch in the header of Runs and Definitions, the same setting as Settings › Execution, per workspace, off by default): while a run is in progress, or a roadmap is running, the app keeps the computer from sleeping (a run waiting at a gate counts only when a roadmap continues after it). The switch shows **Active: 2 runs, roadmap stage 3/6**, **On: waiting for work** or **Off**, and the sidebar shows **Keeping awake** while it holds; the display may still turn off, and choosing Sleep, closing the lid or the Windows power plan still apply
