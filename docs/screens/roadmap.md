The roadmap of the project chosen in the header, on a screen of its own (#254; a tab of the Backlog until then): the ordered stages of runs, **Start** / **Pause** / **Stop**, **Run this stage**, the live roadmap and the Product Owner's drawer, as described under Backlog. A **Planned | All** switch (remembered) chooses what shows: Planned is the open work (every item but the done and skipped ones; a stage's finished items fold under a green **+n done** chip that unfolds that stage only), All is every item. A **date range** (last 24 hours by default, up to Total) filters by each item's last activity: the finished items in Planned (open items always show), every item in All; the header counts "12 of 87 items · last 24 hours". Long roadmaps load in pages as you scroll, starting around the current stage. An item with a run shows the run's chip (the readable status, pulsing while it runs, the workflow and the run's title; a click opens the run), a live timer under its progress ring coloured against its time estimate, and "round 2 · fix round after Code review" when the run was sent back, with the progress it had earned kept. The epic select narrows it to one epic. Links that opened the Backlog's Roadmap tab (a roadmap's notification, the 24/7 hint, the Product Owner's "In roadmap" badges) open this screen
