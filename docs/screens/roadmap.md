The roadmap of the project chosen in the header, on a screen of its own (#254; a tab of the Backlog until then): the ordered stages of runs, **Start** / **Pause** / **Stop**, each stage's **⋮** menu (**Disable** / **Enable**, **Run this stage**, **Release here**), the live roadmap and the Product Owner's drawer, as described under Backlog. A **Planned | All** switch (remembered) chooses what shows: Planned is the open work (every item but the done and skipped ones; a stage's finished items fold under a green **+n done** chip that unfolds that stage only), All is every item. A **date range** (last 24 hours by default, up to Total) filters by each item's last activity: the finished items in Planned (open items always show), every item in All; the header counts "12 of 87 items · last 24 hours". Long roadmaps load in pages as you scroll, starting around the current stage. An item's card (#282) shows its issues, its cost and time against their estimates (the time of a run at work is a live timer, coloured once past its estimate), one status block (the readable status, pulsing while it runs, the current step, and "Round 2 · fix round after Code review" when the run was sent back, with the progress it had earned kept) and a **Workflow** band: the workflow's name with **Open run →**, the mode toggle and the pipeline of the workflow's stations. A disabled stage (#284) stays listed, dimmed, and is skipped while the roadmap runs. The epic select narrows it to one epic. Links that opened the Backlog's Roadmap tab (a roadmap's notification, the 24/7 hint, the Product Owner's "In roadmap" badges) open this screen
