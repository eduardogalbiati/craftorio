The Oracle's night shift (#172). It leads with the switch: **Off**, or **On until 07:30** with who turned it on and when (an end time is required, at most 24 hours; a preset sets 8 h from now). The owner's checklist says what the Oracle may do **without asking** while it is on (retry a failed roadmap item, skip an item, regroup or reorder planned items, start from scratch over kept work, refresh issue text, restore a workflow or skill default, append low-complexity issues, restart the dispatch, commit a workflow or skill fix to the local main, file an issue), each with what it does and its undo; defaults: retry and refresh. Answering a human gate, pushing, closing issues, deleting data and paid calls outside the plan are shown locked, never allowed. Each night is measured from the Oracle's own log (`oracle/night-log.jsonl`) and the runs' events: incidents by kind, autonomy, escalation, resolution, TTD and TTR (median, p90), idle hours by cause, hours saved (an estimate), work unblocked, gate wait, precision (undone actions), loops, false alarms, the watch's cost, the night's yield and the safety counters (a red alarm when a gate was crossed or a never-allowed action ran), a timeline, the actions with their permission, backup and Undo, and a table to compare nights against the baseline night. While the watch is on, the sidebar entry shows a moon and the runs it watches; when it ends, a notification and a card on Home's Overview give the morning summary. Simulated mode can replay a sample night
