The raw ideas of the project chosen in the header, kept as private discovery cards in the workspace (`discovery/`), never on the git platform (#256). **Quick capture**: a title and a text (paste or drop images and files, or **Attach files**; the mic dictates the text), **Enter** captures an `idea` card. The list shows the Inbox cards newest first with author, age and attachment count. A click opens a card next to the list: its document (**Edit**, then **Save** / **Cancel**), its attachments (any file up to 50 MB; an image opens in the viewer) and its thread of notes. Triage: **Accept** (to Discovery › Triage), **Merge into…** another card (the documents and attachments join, the merged card is archived with a link to it), **Discard** (archived with a reason). **Esc** closes the open card. **Import…** brings in a backlog that already exists (#322): a `.md` or `.txt` file (chosen or dropped) or pasted text is cut into items without any model call (each top-level heading, or each top-level list item; its sub-items and paragraphs are its text and its `- [ ]` lines its acceptance criteria; plain prose is one item, with a note). The preview lists each item's title, first lines, number of criteria and the clarity score its card gets, and marks a duplicate of an open card or an open synced issue (keep it, skip it or merge it into that card); rename, **Drop**, **Merge into…** or **Nest under…** items (**Undo** takes the last edit back). Nothing is written until the confirm button: then each item becomes an `idea` card in the Inbox, in the document's order, with its criteria in `acceptance.md`, the source file attached and a history entry naming it; all the cards or none. Over 200 cards it asks first; **Cancel** or **Esc** leaves the workspace unchanged. An import never files an issue: cards reach the platform only through the commitment point
