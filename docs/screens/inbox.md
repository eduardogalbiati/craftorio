The raw ideas of the project chosen in the header, kept as private discovery cards in the workspace (`discovery/`), never on the git platform (#256). **Quick capture**: a title and a text (paste or drop images and files, or **Attach files**; the mic dictates the text), **Enter** captures an `idea` card. The list shows the Inbox cards newest first with author, age and attachment count. A click opens a card next to the list: its document (**Edit**, then **Save** / **Cancel**), its attachments (any file up to 50 MB; an image opens in the viewer) and its thread of notes. Triage: **Accept** (to Discovery › Triage), **Merge into…** another card (the documents and attachments join, the merged card is archived with a link to it), **Discard** (archived with a reason). **Esc** closes the open card
