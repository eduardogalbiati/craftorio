The story

It started with a small question.

"What if I could open several AI sessions at the same time?" Within a few days, the question became a small software factory. And then the most curious part happened: the tool started developing itself.

  1. Day 1

    A weekend idea

    An app that orchestrates Claude Code, Codex and Grok in a real development flow, with cross-review, tests and a gate where only I decide.

  2. Day 2

    The first real runs

    One AI implements, another from a different family reviews, a third writes the tests. And a supervisor that only steps in when something fails.

  3. Day 4

    The tool takes the wheel

    I wrote down the backlog, and the issues started being implemented by the tool itself. From then on, I followed along instead of writing code.

  4. Day 5

    The first full night

    An AI Product Owner laid out the plan in stages, and the roadmap ran until morning. I went to sleep and woke up to 10 tasks delivered, tested and merged.

  5. Today

    A factory that never sleeps

    By day I say the ideas, by night they become code. A night watch unblocks what it can, within what I allowed, and leaves the decisions to me.
