Home follows the owner's redesign. "What needs you" leads, with chips counting what waits and what failed, the robot of
who asks and one button per row, and it now also lists a PR that needs the owner (Open PRs), a new production error
(Diagnose) and the day's budget at 80 % or more (Open Usage). The flow reads as the Roadmap's stations, every stage's
whole name, the running stage in blue and the one to push in amber. Three blocks show only while they have something:
Now (the project's runs in progress), Roadmap (the current stage as a bar) and Next release (what merged locally, with
the release checks). "Last watch" (the last run of the Night watch, by day or by night, kept for a week, its figures in
the interface language) sits beside "Last 24 hours", which replaces "Today" and drops the next roadmap stage. The
Oracle stays docked, smaller, with a one-line introduction; its field and Send follow the interface language; under
860 px it goes below Home's content. Nothing in Home has a fixed width any more.
