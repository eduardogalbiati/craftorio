The Roadmap draws a stage's items as lanes: one group per workflow, the workflow's stations as columns named once, and
one row per run with a glyph per station (the station the run is at is a pill that opens the run), a progress ring and
two bars for cost and time against the estimate. A stage's head counts its items in chips (waiting for you, failed,
running, done) and "Show the stage on the map" moved into its ⋮ menu. A row opens to its details: the cost, time and
progress meters, the run's summary, Open run, the mode, the ending, the earlier runs, Edit, move and Remove. The
Roadmap's header no longer repeats the page's title and shows its four numbers as wells.
