A project with no remote gets a local backlog: its issues live in the workspace (`backlog:create-issue`,
`backlog:update-issue`, `backlog:close-issue`), the Product Owner and a Discovery commit file into it, and
`backlog:file-local` previews filing them on the platform once a repository is added (nothing is filed
without the owner's click).
